Delete your Risaina account

Risaina, by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} · Last updated: 29 October 2026

You can delete your Risaina account and its data at any time, in the app or by email. Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting your account also deletes what you shared with your partner. Risaina's chat, photos, voice notes, memories, lists, dates, calendar, shared alarms and games belong to the two of you together. When either partner deletes their account, the pairing ends and all of this is deleted for both of you. If you want a copy, export it first (Settings → Privacy → “Export my data (JSON)”).

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Risaina and sign in.
  2. On Home, tap Settings (the gear at the top), then tap Privacy. If you have not paired with a partner yet, tap Privacy & account at the bottom of the pairing screen instead.
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password. If you signed up with Google and have no password, type DELETE.
  5. Tap Delete forever. Your account is deleted straight away and every phone is signed out.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the subject “Delete my Risaina account”.

  • Send it from the email address you use for Risaina, so we know the request is yours. If that is not possible, tell us the address and we will send a confirmation to it first.
  • We delete the account, exactly as the in-app option does, within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

  • A message you sent: long-press it in the chat and tap Delete. Its text, photo or voice note is removed for both of you.
  • A memory you added, a list, a calendar entry or a shared alarm: open it and tap the Delete (bin) icon.
  • Your live location: stop sharing. Your position is deleted from the server at once.
  • Everything you share as a couple: Settings → Unlink. This deletes all shared data for both of you, and keeps your account.
  • Profile details: clear your birthday or city in your profile, and switch off time and weather sharing in Settings → Privacy.
  • Or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and tell us what you want deleted.
