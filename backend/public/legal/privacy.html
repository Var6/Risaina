Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Applies to the Risaina Android app (package com.risaina.app) and the Risaina server it talks to.

Risaina is a private app for two people in a relationship. It is run by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} (“we”, “us”). This policy explains, in plain words, what the app collects, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. If anything here is unclear, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • We collect what Risaina needs to work: your account details, what you send to your partner, and a few technical details about your phone so notifications arrive.
  • Only you and the one partner you pair with can see your content. We do not sell data, show ads, use analytics or track you across other apps.
  • Your location is collected only while you choose to share it. We keep only your latest position and delete it when sharing stops. There is no location history.

  • You can export your data, unlink (which deletes everything the two of you shared) and delete your account from inside the app.

2. What stays only on your phone

  • Your partner's phone number, if you add it in Settings → Safety. It never leaves your phone. It is used only for the “Call” and “Text” buttons when an SOS is not getting through, and those open your own phone and messaging apps.
  • A local copy of your chat, games and widget content, and cached photos and voice notes, so the app and widgets work quickly and offline. They are kept in Risaina's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. The chat, games and widget content are cleared when you sign out, unlink or delete your account. Cached photo and voice-note files stay in Risaina's private storage until you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Risaina → Storage → Clear storage).
  • Appearance settings (colour, dark mode, home background).
  • The data export file, which the app saves to your phone's Downloads folder when you ask for it.

3. What your partner can see

Risaina connects exactly two accounts. Your partner can see:

  • Your display name and profile photo, and your birthday (without the year if you hide it).
  • Your local time and your city's weather, only while those switches are on (Settings → Privacy → “Your partner sees”).
  • Everything you send or save in the shared space: messages, photos, voice notes, pings, gifts, memories, notes, lists, dates, widget notes, games and their results.
  • When your phone received and read their messages and pings.
  • Your live location, accuracy, battery level and last update time, only while you are sharing.
  • The status of an SOS: whether it rang on the other phone and when it was acknowledged.
  • For troubleshooting: your phone model, app version, when your phone last received a push, the status codes listed in section 1, and the phone's alarm and ringer state when an SOS rang on it.

Nobody else can see your content. There are no public profiles, no search for other users and no group chats.

4. Live location

  • Live location is off until you turn it on (Settings → Panic button & SOS → “Share my live location”), or until you send a panic alert or an SOS with “Also share my live location” switched on.
  • Only you can start it, from a screen you are looking at. Your partner cannot request it or switch it on. Risaina does not have background location permission.
  • A notification with a “Stop sharing” button shows on your phone the whole time. Sharing you start yourself cannot begin without it. A panic alert or an SOS still shares if you have turned off Risaina's notifications, because an emergency must not be blocked; the app tells you no notification will show, and you can stop sharing in the app.
  • You choose how long: 1 hour, 8 hours, or until you turn it off. A panic alert shares for 2 hours unless you stop earlier or tap “I'm safe now”.
  • You can give Android “approximate” instead of “precise” location; your partner then sees an area of about 2 km.
  • The server keeps only your latest position, never a history. It is deleted as soon as you stop, when the time runs out, when you sign out, unlink or delete your account, or after 12 hours without an update. After you stop, a marker without any coordinates is kept for up to 10 minutes so that a delayed “start” cannot restart sharing.
  • Positions are sent to your partner's phone live and are never written into notifications, push messages, the sync log or server logs. Your data export contains only your own current position, if you are sharing at that moment.
  • Your phone's location is determined by Google Play services (the fused location provider) on your phone. Google's handling of location settings on your phone is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

5. SOS and the panic button

An SOS rings your partner's phone like an alarm. To do that, our server sends a high-priority push through Firebase Cloud Messaging. The push contains only identifiers, never your message or location. Your partner's phone then fetches the SOS from our server. If your partner's phone does not confirm the SOS, the server sends the push again, at most once a minute and up to 6 times, during the first 10 minutes. When the SOS rings, the receiving phone reports its alarm volume, ringer mode, Do Not Disturb state and similar technical details (no content), so that a missed alarm can be explained.

Risaina is not an emergency service and cannot contact the police, an ambulance or anyone other than your partner. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

8. Security

  • All connections use TLS encryption (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). The release app refuses unencrypted connections.
  • The database and file storage are private and require credentials that only the server holds. Files are reachable only through short-lived signed links, and only by the two members of a couple.
  • Passwords are stored as argon2id hashes. Sign-in tokens are short-lived and stored only as hashes on the server.
  • On your phone, sign-in data and cached content are kept in Risaina's private storage and are excluded from cloud backup.
  • View-once photos are blocked from screenshots on the receiving phone and cannot be downloaded a second time.
  • Limits: content is not end-to-end encrypted. Encryption at rest is provided by MongoDB Atlas and Cloudflare. Someone with access to the server, which is us, can technically read stored content. We only access it when needed to run, secure or fix the service, or if the law requires it.

9. How long we keep data, and deletion

While you use Risaina

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Shared content (messages, photos, voice notes, pings, gifts, memories, lists, dates, games, SOS alerts): until you delete it, until you or your partner unlinks, or until either of you deletes your account.
  • Deleting a chat message you sent (long-press, then Delete) removes its text and any photo or voice note for both of you, and deletes the file from storage. A record that a message was sent, and when, remains in the chat.
  • Sync log: so that a phone that was offline can catch up, the server keeps a log of changes in your shared space (for example “new message”, with its content, or “list updated”, with the list). This log is kept until you unlink or one of you deletes your account. This means the original text of a deleted message, and earlier versions of lists and profile details, stay in the log until then. Live location is never part of this log.
  • Deleting a memory or a list hides it for both of you at once. A hidden copy stays on the server until you unlink or delete your account. A photo you remove from Memories stays in the chat until you delete that message.
  • Deleting a date or countdown removes it for both of you at once. Earlier entries about it in the sync log stay until you unlink or delete your account.
  • View-once photos can be opened only once: the link to the photo works for 2 minutes, and the file is deleted from storage within about 20 minutes after it is opened, or after 7 days if never opened. A placeholder stays in the chat.
  • Live location: see section 4. Only the latest position, deleted when sharing stops.
  • Uploads that were never sent are deleted after 24 hours.
  • Device records and push tokens: until you sign out on that phone or delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: ended when you sign out. The session records are deleted automatically 7 days after they expire (sessions last at most 30 days).
  • Email codes and password reset links: stored only as hashes, deleted within about a day after they expire.
  • Server logs: kept by Render for a limited period, at most 30 days.
  • Backups: We do not keep database backups. Deleted data is gone from the database at once.

When you unlink

Either partner can unlink at any time (Settings → Unlink). Unlinking ends the pairing for both of you and immediately deletes everything the couple created: messages, pings, gifts, memories, dates, lists, games, widget notes, SOS alerts, invitations, the sync log and both people's live location. Photos, voice notes, chat wallpapers and profile photos uploaded while you were paired are made unavailable at once and deleted from storage in the background, normally within an hour. What remains: a record that the pairing existed (the two internal account IDs and the start and end dates, without names or content), and a single “unlinked” notice that lets the other phone clean up, deleted after 7 days. This cannot be undone, so export your data first if you want a copy.

When you delete your account

See our account deletion page for the steps. In short: deleting your account unlinks you (with everything described above, for both of you), signs out every phone, removes your devices and push tokens, live location, codes and widget notes, and deletes your files. Your email address, name, password, Google link, birthday, time zone, city and profile photo are erased. We keep only an anonymous record with an internal ID, dates and your notification settings, so that internal references stay consistent; it cannot be linked back to you. Revoked session records are deleted within about 37 days, and server logs within the period stated above.

11. Children

Risaina is meant for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child is using Risaina, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will delete the account.

12. Where data is stored

Our server runs on Render, the database on MongoDB Atlas and files on Cloudflare R2, in data centres these providers run, which may be outside your country. Google's Firebase services may process push tokens in other countries. This means your data may be stored or processed outside the country where you live. We use only providers that protect data with industry-standard security.
