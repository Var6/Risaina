# Privacy policy

The published policy is `backend/public/legal/privacy.html`, served by the backend at `/privacy`
(https://risaina.onrender.com/privacy). The account-deletion page Google Play requires is
`backend/public/legal/delete-account.html`, served at `/delete-account`. The developer name and contact
email in both pages come from `LEGAL_CONTACT` in `backend/src/modules/legal/routes.ts`.

Keep the policy in step with the code: when what the app collects, who receives it or how long it is kept
changes, update the page, its "Last updated" date, and the Data safety answers in Play Console.
